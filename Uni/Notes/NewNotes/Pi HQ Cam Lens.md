#### Definition
The lens we want for the project is the [100mm lens](100mm telephoto lens, C-mount) but that is not available right now, so we use a 50mm lens.

At 50m distance the 50mm lens will have an fov of: H7.2 and V5.4, resulting in 7.54x5.65 m.
At 50m distance the 100mm lens will have an fov of: H3.6 and V2.7 resulting in 3.77x2.83 m.
